# Meeting formats — report (2026-11-26)

## What works on prod (simonsays.harrysson.co, v1 a86ab93, migrations 0023–0027)

- **Agenda mode:** start a meeting in the ordförande's own agenda Doc. Simon Says suggests the Doc from the meeting folder; the starter confirms or picks another with the Google Picker. Notes are written live under the right agenda item, tasks by role in "Vad ska vi göra till nästa gång?", "Från förra veckan" pre-filled from last meeting's open tasks (and "✓ klart" flows back), Må-runda kept out of the notes, Närvarande/Tid filled, "Simon Says · avslutat" at the end. Human edits always win.
- **No agenda:** a new Doc in the förening's format.
- **Formats:** Veckomöte, Styrelseprotokoll (with stadgar: upload them and Simon Says adds the required items), Enkel. Or "Lär Simon Says ert format" from a template or old minutes (only structure leaves the Doc), or "Anpassa format" to edit the current one (e.g. remove Må-runda).
- **Roles:** an admin can give each person a role ("Kassör"); tasks are written by role.
- Checked by three Testmöten on prod with a fictional Veckomöte agenda, and a paid eval: placement 20/20, roles 14/14, Från förra veckan 8/8, Må-runda leaks 0, $1.18 per meeting hour.

## What to try at Hook & Look's first real meeting

1. Before: on the förening page, give the board their roles (Ordförande, Kassör, …), choose Veckomöte (or "Lär Simon Says ert format" with the Mall Doc), and set the meeting folder.
2. The ordförande writes the agenda Doc as usual in that folder.
3. At the table: "Starta möte" → it should suggest today's agenda Doc → "Använd" → consent → name round ("Jag heter …") → Må-runda.
4. Watch the Doc: notes appear under the item being discussed; type freely — your edits win.
5. After: check with the secretary what was missing or placed wrong; write it in docs/learnings.md.

## Open (parked, not blocking)

- One anchored non-list line can switch a list agenda into bold mode for the meeting.
- A stray empty bullet can remain above a table after we remove our line.
- If two people race to start in the same Doc, the loser's markers stay in the Doc.
- A mixed agenda (numbered then bulleted) merges the bulleted part into the last numbered item.
- Styrelseprotokoll placement was 9/11 in one of two eval runs.
