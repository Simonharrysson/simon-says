# Three audiences — proposal (todos #11, #14; #12 later). NOT approved.

Simon: offer Simon Says to individuals (Privat), companies (Företag) and associations (Föreningar); "make each of these 3 very custom so users feel like the system helps them"; inspiration: his own recorder Pocket AI.

## Names
sv: Privat · Företag · Föreningar. en: Personal · Business · Associations ("for clubs, student unions and non-profits"). One account can belong to several spaces.

## What Pocket AI teaches (how Simon records as an individual)
- Starts by hand (menu bar / one button / phone), records customer meetings, association meetings and "Övrigt" (lectures, group work).
- The product is the e-mail ~10 min after: summary, decisions, to-dos per owner, open questions, "Sedan sist" (last time's to-dos: done / open / not mentioned), read-aloud link, "Fel kontext?" fix.
- Contexts (customer / association / courses) auto-detected from the calendar overlapping the recording, attendees, title, course codes.
- Company flavour: customer matched from attendees, "our / their" to-dos, CRM updated with undo, follow-up mail drafts.

## Proposal per space
**Föreningar** (today): live protocol in the shared Doc, agenda/Förbered möte, roles, goals, invites, summaries to absentees. Free.

**Privat** — "your own secretary": record anything (meeting, lecture, call) from the browser/phone; no shared Doc required (a private Doc optional); main output = Pocket-style e-mail + a personal page: summary, my to-dos, "Sedan sist", read-aloud; contexts (courses, projects) auto-detected from your calendar; later: search/ask over your own history. Free (or limited free hours).

**Företag** — "the meeting never got lost": team space; meetings matched to customers via calendar attendees; to-dos split "vi / kunden"; follow-up e-mail draft to the customer (never sent automatically); notes to the company's Docs/Drive; later: CRM export (HubSpot/Pipedrive). Paid via credits (#12, only companies pay).

**Calendar (#14)**: Google Calendar read-only, per user. Shows upcoming meetings on the start page; one click "Förbered" / "Starta" from an event (title, date, attendees prefilled as Närvarande); classifies Privat/Företag/Förening context after a recording. Needs the calendar.readonly scope (Google "sensitive", verification before >100 users).

## Landing / copy
Three doors on the landing page ("Privat", "Företag", "Förening"), each with its own one-line promise and screenshots; the app adapts navigation and words per space (e.g. "förening/medlemmar" vs "team/kolleger" vs nothing).
