## Wave 1 — before a real förening uses it (parallel where files don't overlap)

1. **Meeting continuity** (L1, L8, U7, U8): closing the laptop without Avsluta → meeting "abandoned" after 15 min and its tasks never carry forward / reach Översikt; only the starter can resume/end; no admin "Avsluta"; "Logga in igen" mid-meeting offers "Fortsätt mötet" and takes over the live tab.
2. **Docs live in the förening, not in the starter's Drive** (L9, U5, U6): new Docs ignore the meeting folder; members who join later get no access; members see "Dokument" links that open Google's access-denied page.
3. **Agenda flow + prepare mode** (Simon #6, U2, U9, C2, L6, L7): separate "Förbered möte" mode for the ordförande (Simon Says creates next meeting's agenda Doc in the meeting folder in advance → can also be suggested, fixing the drive.file gap); prove "Från förra veckan" end to end; agenda step asks "Har ni en dagordning?" with two equal buttons; Doc edits after a meeting (✓ klart, owners, decisions) are read back; task chain survives a meeting without the item; "Starta möte" copy matches the mode.
4. **Spend & abuse** (L2, L4): the $25/day Claude cap is global → per-förening caps and rate limits on learn-format/stadgar too; the 2-orgs-per-user limit counts deleted orgs.
5. **Admin roles** (L3): make someone admin / hand over; last admin cannot leave → board turnover gets stuck.
6. **Sign-in & invites** (U1, U10): invite link lost when Google sign-in fails (callback drops `next`); copy for Google's unverified-app warning; sign-in trust copy.
7. **Privacy** (U4, L10, L-M10): Må-runda masked in the notes but verbatim in the shared transcript tab; /integritet vs product; transcript retention/export.
8. **Ops checklist step 0** (L5): Resend key (Simon), remove localhost from Supabase redirects, /design preview off.
